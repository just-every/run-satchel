# Client portal, timeline and scheduled summaries

## The matter timeline and search

**Matters → Matter timeline** merges notes, calls, client commitments, filed email, documents, tasks, deadlines, appointments, bills and payments you may see, newest first, each linked to its screen. Filter by type, text or mentions of you. A note is **private** (readable by its author alone, not even the principal) or visible to everyone who sees the matter; only the principal or a lawyer can share a team entry with the client. Mentions name a person who can already see the matter and grant nothing. Entries are never edited: a correction is a new entry that cites the old one.

The search box in the workspace header finds matters (by name, number or case number), contacts and aliases, notes, tasks, events, documents, email, invoices and payments, grouped by type. Press `/` to focus it, use the arrow keys to move through the results and Enter to open one. Results apply matter access, custom roles and ethical walls before any title, count or snippet is returned.

## Inviting clients

The principal shares a matter from **Team & safety → Client portal sharing**, choosing a kind of access: **client**, **co-client**, **payer** (issued invoices and their PDFs only) or **delegate** (acts for one named client and loses access when that client's access ends). **Preview what … sees** runs that person's own timeline, shared items, invoices, message threads and document-request doors and is audited. Previewing does not mark the person's messages read. A delegate also loses access while the client they represent is inactive.

Counsel then shares exact records: **Matters → Shared with the client** shares a task, event, confirmed deadline or document. The client can acknowledge each and mark a shared task done; that never completes the firm's task or changes a date. **Document requests** ask for a specific record with instructions and acceptance criteria; the client uploads, counsel accepts exactly one upload (filing it as the matter's original) or explains what is missing. **Client messages** keep two-way threads with attachments, read states and an unread queue; the notification email says only that a message is waiting.

Threads are assigned only to active staff who can currently see the matter. If the assigned person loses access, later client messages stay in the retained thread and no notification is sent to that person; counsel can assign a current reader to take over the unread work.

Email notifications contain no message content and are sent only to current readers, after the principal records the mail provider review in **Provider charges**.

## Scheduled summaries

**Reports → Scheduled summaries** lets the principal schedule a weekly workload or deadlines summary to named recipients, by email or inside Satchel. Each summary carries counts only and is built with the recipient's own report permission when it is sent. A failed delivery opens an exception for the principal.

## Named financial releases

Ordinary shared-item access does not release a trust statement or replenishment request. Clients and co-clients are equally eligible, but the principal must release each exact signed statement separately to each named current grant. Payers and delegates receive neither. The current workspace has no financial-release or client trust controls; the documented API doors provide the bounded workflow while TRUST-008 remains partial.

A principal with current trust-record authority and full account access can release a current officer-signed statement. The frozen release retains its original cutoff even after later postings or corrected packets. A new release must name the current signed version. Withdrawal ends access without deleting the signed record or its audit.

The principal approves versioned retainer thresholds. A principal who also holds the designated trust-officer capability can approve an exact posted-balance crossing and publish a replenishment request. A sole practitioner may hold both capabilities. Publication is availability inside the authenticated portal, separate from access or acknowledgment. It sends no email or SMS, calls no payment provider and posts no debit.

Every financial read rechecks the original grant, current authorizing principal, required trust capability, full account access and ethical walls. Replacing a withdrawn grant does not revive an old release. Native archives preserve signed records, policy versions and audit evidence, and restore no financial publication authority. State-record downloads remain incomplete firm-review previews; they certify no compliance or retention period.
