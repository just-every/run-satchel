# Free software and provider charges

Satchel is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no Satchel license fee, subscription, paywall, seat charge or matter limit, and the app never asks for a payment, plan or license key. You run it on your own computer or server, including your own Cloudflare account. [Get started](getting-started.md) shows the run command.

"100% free" describes Satchel software. Providers you connect bill your firm directly under their own terms: payment providers such as LawPay and Stripe, e-signature providers such as DocuSign and Inkwell, accounting providers such as QuickBooks Online and Xero, and your hosting, mail, SMS, court-rule and filing providers. Satchel adds no charge, markup or resale fee to any of them.

## Before you connect a provider

The principal opens **Team & safety**, then **Provider charges**. Each provider is listed with the statement that it bills the firm directly and that Satchel's charge is none. For a provider, enter the address of the provider's own terms page, tick that you inspected them, and choose **Record provider review**. The review is recorded with your name and the time in the action audit.

Satchel connects a provider only after that review. Until the principal records it, the connection is refused and the message names the provider. Later provider features, including payments, e-signature, accounting and SMS follow-up, use the same check.
