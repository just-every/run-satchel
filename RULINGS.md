# Satchel rulings

### RL-1 — Separate finance and legal rights

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Ruled by:** ct  
**Governs:** FIRM-002

Billing staff draft bills and, once a lawyer or the principal has approved one, issue and export it; they cannot approve, reach trust records, conflicts, membership or matter access. Accountants read their assigned matters, billing and every trust record and reconcile (import a statement period, record bank clearance, match checks) but cannot authorize, sign or reopen a period or change anything else. A client reaches no staff door: only the principal shares one matter at a time with an active client member, with a recorded reason and an audit entry, and the client then sees that matter’s number, title and status; a walled matter is never shown and withdrawing the share closes the view. Agent keys and integrations need a principal, lawyer, assistant or trust-officer sponsor, so a member whose role changes to billing, accountant or client loses any earlier key at once. FIRM-008’s custom roles narrow these roles and never widen them; CLIENT-001 to CLIENT-009 own portal identities, content and messaging beyond this view and inherit the share and withdrawal rules.

### RL-2 — Enforce ethical walls

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Ruled by:** ct  
**Governs:** FIRM-003

The principal establishes a wall naming an active lawyer or principal custodian, who is always a wall member; for a wall INTAKE-010 creates at opening, the default custodian is the lawyer who records the restriction. After creation only the current custodian changes membership, hands custodianship to another active lawyer or principal inside the wall, or dissolves the wall, each with a recorded reason; nobody outside the wall, the principal and administrators included, adds themselves, changes members or custodian, approves access or dissolves it. With no active custodian the wall stays in force, reads "custodian unassigned", allows no exceptional access, and an active lawyer or principal inside the wall may take custodianship with a recorded reason. Exceptional access is requested with a reason by one active person in a native session (never an agent key, integration, portal user or client contact). Only the current custodian approves or refuses it, with a decision note. It lasts at most 60 minutes from approval and never renews: a further need is a new request. It is revoked early by the custodian or by any membership or custodian change. It opens the walled matter's current doors for that person only. It never confers membership or custodian authority, never reaches their agent keys or integrations, and never counts toward the every-wall permission for whole-firm archives, import originals or account aggregates. Request, decision, expiry, revocation and the recipient's walled reads under a grant are FIRM-005 audit actions readable inside the wall; the recipient sees their own request, decision and expiry. INTAKE-010's walled routing and the walls its restrictions create are proved under INTAKE-010.

### RL-3 — Review conflicts across the whole firm

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Ruled by:** ct  
**Governs:** INTAKE-005

Only the principal and lawyers (SAT-002, FIRM-002) search, dispose, record waivers and set engagement restrictions; assistants, trust officers, agents and portal users cannot. Besides current and former clients, matches are labelled *prospective client* for a contact on a lead (INTAKE-002) or consultation (INTAKE-003) not converted, with its stage and firm-local year; *related entity* through INTAKE-004 links; and *staff relationship* for a relationship the principal or a lawyer recorded between a staff member and a contact (kind, dates, source), shown to the principal and lawyers only. Labels are recorded associations, never determinations. A match whose matter role the searcher cannot read under current grants or private-record rules is a *restricted match*: the searcher sees the matched contact and alias as INTAKE-004 allows and, in place of every hidden matter, role, title and count, one route per custodian ("Restricted match: routed to <custodian>"). Until FIRM-003 ships the custodian is the principal, the current native custodian (FIRM-005, FIRM-006). The custodian records a disposition from a closed set (no conflict; waivable, waiver recorded; proceed with engagement restrictions; decline) bound to that review's revision; the searcher sees only the outcome, custodian, time and any restriction text the custodian writes for disclosure. Opening is refused until every route has a current custodian disposition, no route says decline, every waivable match has a waiver and counsel's overall disposition is recorded; any party change makes all of them stale (SAT-002). A waiver records the waiving contacts (each holding a client role on the affected matter), date, scope text and the match it answers, with a file or a written confirmation, labelled "practitioner-recorded, not signed through Satchel" until INTAKE-006 supplies signatures; a restricted match's waiver is recorded and read only by its custodian. Engagement restrictions attach to the new matter at opening: disclosed condition text on the matter header for everyone with access, and excluded non-principal staff whom SAT-003's grant door refuses, naming the restriction; removing one needs the principal or a lawyer on the matter with a reason. Every route, disposition, waiver and restriction is a FIRM-005 audit action, travels in archives, and is retained, held and disposed with FIRM-006's retained conflict originals. Retained restriction payload disposal uses FIRM-006: the principal types the search reference and records a reason, every affected source and opened matter has an expired schedule and no hold, and saved counsel notes, condition text, acknowledgment text and their audit payload copies are replaced by a disposal marker. Stable ids, decisions, attribution, times and original audit digests remain. Existing staff exclusions and walls stay enforced; a disposed review cannot clear an engagement or accept a new disposition. New searches are required. Search, routing and disposition are evidence for counsel, never legal clearance; applying the conflict rules is the firm's.

### RL-101 — BILL-007: Reconcile payments refunds and disputes

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Ruled by:** ct  
**Governs:** BILL-007  
**Outcome:** adopt with rider

**Question:** May principal-approved exact operating refunds reconcile uncertainty without automatically repeating the charge-side effect?

**Default proposed/adopted:** Active principal approves exact invoice revision, cents and reason; append-only history, no uncertain retry, authenticated readback and exact payout/statement match.

**Clause outcome:** Require explicit finance authority and exact refund approval, keep uncertain outcomes unresolved until authenticated reconciliation and never show bank settlement without matching completed payout and statement evidence.

**Why:** Clio refunds completed payments and distinguishes refund from recorded manual disbursement. The principal-only mapping is Satchel's current finance authority, not a universal prohibition on delegated finance staff.

**Placement:** BILL-007 owns refund/reconciliation policy; preserve full finance delegation, provider events, fee/ledger posting and bank-match reversal scope. Missing Stripe credentials are provider proof work.
