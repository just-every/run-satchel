# Satchel product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Satchel is the US small-firm practice management system a Clio Manage, MyCase, Smokeball or PracticePanther buyer can switch to, with Filevine’s matter-work depth and source-grounded assistance beyond today’s Eve workflows. It runs intake and conflicts, engagements, matters, documents and email, court deadlines, client communication, time capture, billing, built-in trust accounting, legal payments and collections.

This is the product being built towards. Missing implementation is a gap, never a reason to narrow the product. Each named clause describes buyer behavior and an acceptance journey through a public screen, route, download, command or real provider boundary. Current status is judged separately from that target. ACCEPTANCE.md lists the same journeys and statuses for build rounds. No product code, approved homepage, UI or behavior changes in this documentation round.

## Whole job and authority

Satchel owns the legal practice loop from prospective client through closed matter and reconciled money. Trust accounting is built in: client subsidiary ledgers, bank registers and three-way reconciliation are core product obligations, not a link to an external spreadsheet. Banks and contracted processors hold and move actual money. Counsel retains conflict clearance, legal judgments, deadline confirmation and external filing authority. Agents prepare and act only under explicit delegated nonlegal authority; people retain legal and financial approvals.

The published foundation had no root CONTRACT.md or named clause IDs. SAT-001 through SAT-013 give stable names to its existing ACCEPTANCE.md promises, preserving the behavior. Their 1 MiB implementation limit and old exclusions of delivered invoices, trust accounting and provider calls describe the prior release, not target boundaries. New target clauses supersede those scope exclusions while preserving original-byte integrity, review history and authority checks. USD cents remain exact; foreign-currency invoices require explicit agreements and separate currency ledgers.

Neighbouring products remain separate. [Chest](https://github.com/just-every/run-chest) owns general-purpose file collaboration; Satchel owns matter evidence and connects repositories. [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) supplies signatures, alongside DocuSign; Satchel owns engagement and matter workflow. [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) is a general accounting product; Satchel owns legal billing and IOLTA subsidiary accounting and connects QuickBooks Online/Xero. General CRM, enterprise e-discovery and unrestricted legal research databases remain separate products; matter intake, scoped research integration and evidence production remain Satchel obligations.

All clauses inherit server-side authorization, matter confidentiality, stable source identities, append-only posted financial records, reviewed exact revisions, durable job state and explicit uncertain outcomes. Native Base session/import/portability guarantees continue through BASE-001. A fresh production installation contains no sample clients. Examples in acceptance journeys are test inputs, never seeded production records. Paid/network services may use official sandboxes at the edge; application workflows must use real application handlers.

## Published foundations

- **SAT-001 — Invite a firm team.** The principal saves and invites lawyers and assistants, deactivates membership and controls matter grants. Private handlers check the native session, active firm role and matter access themselves; a hidden button is never the boundary. **Acceptance evidence:** Through Team and the JSON API invite an assistant, grant one matter, read it, then revoke membership; direct private reads and writes fail immediately.

- **SAT-002 — Review fresh conflicts before opening.** A principal or lawyer searches firm parties, adverse parties and aliases, reviews recorded matches and writes a conclusion before opening a matter. Changed party records invalidate the search. Search is evidence for counsel, never a guarantee of legal clearance. **Acceptance evidence:** Run a conflict search, add another matter, attempt opening against the stale search and receive refusal; repeat with fresh review and explicit confirmation to open.

- **SAT-003 — Grant matter access.** The principal grants or removes access to individual matters. Other staff see only assigned matters; the principal can see all. Finance and ethical-wall extensions in FIRM-002 and FIRM-003 add narrower authority without claiming they exist today. **Acceptance evidence:** As an unassigned assistant request a matter and its billing, files and deadlines directly, receive refusal, then grant and remove access and repeat.

- **SAT-004 — Record exact USD work.** Authorized staff record dated matter time in whole minutes and hourly USD cents, or disbursements in USD cents. Charges use exact integer arithmetic with declared half-up rounding to cents; invalid dates and unsafe amounts are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Record 90 minutes at $250 per hour and a $25 disbursement through Time & bills; inspect $400 in the prepared package and reject an invalid date.

- **SAT-005 — Review immutable bill exports.** A lawyer prepares a bill from unbilled matter entries, reviews the exact revision and exports its recorded CSV artifact. Source reservations prevent double billing; assistants and agents cannot approve, and an exported artifact cannot be voided or overwritten. **Acceptance evidence:** Reserve entries in a draft, refuse another bill using them, refuse premature export and assistant approval, approve as counsel and download the immutable CSV.

- **SAT-006 — Correct by reviewed additions.** A correction is a separately reviewed delta referencing the original bill and source amounts. The original exported artifact, review identity and history remain intact. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and approve a courtesy reduction through the billing API; export its negative delta and download the original again with identical bytes.

- **SAT-007 — Keep private original versions.** Matter documents retain original bytes, hashes, version metadata and creator history. Matter visibility follows access; private visibility is uploader/principal only. Downloads validate integrity and use safe attachment headers. The current implementation limit is 1 MiB; DOC-001 widens the target. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload private and matter-visible originals, refuse the assistant private download, append a version and retrieve both originals through authenticated file routes.

- **SAT-008 — Confirm source-linked deadlines.** A deadline has a date, IANA timezone, source citation and optional exact document version. A lawyer confirms and completes it with evidence. A changed source version supersedes earlier live deadlines and requires fresh review. **Acceptance evidence:** Propose and confirm a cited deadline, append its source version, refuse completion of the superseded date, then confirm a replacement and record delivery evidence.

- **SAT-009 — Let agents prepare for people.** Revocable agent credentials read only within their human sponsor’s access and prepare matter, bill and deadline proposals. A person approves or declines once; approving a proposal does not substitute for conflict, bill or deadline review. **Acceptance evidence:** Use an agent key to prepare each proposal, refuse agent approval and repeated human decisions, check the human decision record, then revoke the key and refuse access.

- **SAT-010 — Preview Clio contact move-in.** The principal previews the documented Clio contacts CSV through Base import-kit, commits its exact fingerprint, resumes and inspects the provenance journal. Replay reuses the job; undo removes unchanged unused contacts and preserves native matter links. Full Clio migration is MOVE-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload fixtures/clio-contacts.csv through Move in, prove preview writes nothing, refuse a changed fingerprint, apply and replay, open a linked matter and undo while preserving its contact.

- **SAT-011 — Restore a private firm archive.** The principal exports the complete current native firm archive and restores only into empty product data after integrity validation. Product records, review history and import provenance round-trip; identity, sessions, local configuration and agent keys remain excluded. **Acceptance evidence:** Export through Team & safety, refuse corrupt and nonempty restore, restore into an empty installation, export again and compare all native rows and original bill bytes.

- **SAT-012 — Erase originals with a restore fence.** A principal confirms a document name and reason to erase every version’s bytes while preserving metadata, hashes and the erasure journal. Supplying the later journal fences older archives; copies already downloaded or delivered remain outside local erasure. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase through the document API, refuse assistant erasure, confirm all version downloads fail and reject a pre-erasure backup when accompanied by the later journal.

- **SAT-013 — Keep the chosen product surfaces.** The public site retains Run Satchel at /run and navigable Markdown guides at /docs. The installed root remains the Lobby; sign-in, setup and all workspace tabs retain the picked theme and available actions at desktop and phone widths. **Acceptance evidence:** Navigate the built site’s run instructions and guides; at 1440 and 390 pixels complete sign-in, setup and each workspace tab with real matter records, checking actions and role restrictions.

## Target clauses

### Firm authority and records

- **FIRM-001 — Set up an empty US firm.** A fresh installation starts with the buyer’s firm, offices, US contact details, practice areas, IANA timezone, USD billing defaults and fiscal settings entered by the principal or imported with provenance; production never starts with demo clients. **Acceptance evidence:** Start a fresh installation, finish setup, inspect empty matters and enter firm settings; export and restore them.

- **FIRM-002 — Separate finance and legal rights.** Principal manages membership and policies; lawyers clear conflicts, confirm legal deadlines and approve legal work; assistants manage assigned work without legal approval; billing staff draft and deliver approved bills; trust officers authorize transfers; accountants reconcile with read-only matter context; clients see explicit portal grants. **Acceptance evidence:** Create each role and use direct APIs for legal approval, invoice delivery, trust transfer, reconciliation and access changes; deny every capability outside that role.

- **FIRM-003 — Enforce ethical walls.** Restricted matters support explicit ethical walls, excluding ordinary administrators and search, exports, notifications, connected repositories and agent context. A designated custodian records exceptional access with reason and an audit entry. **Acceptance evidence:** Wall off an adverse matter, search and export as a principal outside the wall, then approve a documented emergency access and inspect its trace.

- **FIRM-004 — Secure sessions and connected secrets.** Native sign-in is extended with MFA or federated SSO, session/device revocation and least-privilege connector grants. Secrets stay installation-local, encrypted at rest, redacted from logs and absent from archives; revocation stops new work and exposes in-flight uncertainty. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in with MFA, revoke a device and a Microsoft connector while work is queued, then attempt reads and inspect logs and an export.

- **FIRM-005 — Export a complete action audit.** Authorized custodians filter and export an append-only history of actor, role, matter, source, revision, human decision, automation policy and provider outcome. Audit access respects walls; corrections add records rather than rewriting prior actions. **Acceptance evidence:** Open the audit, filter a matter and date range, export a transfer and approval trail, and verify an unauthorized reader cannot inspect it.

- **FIRM-006 — Apply retention and legal holds.** Counsel assigns matter/document retention schedules and legal holds; holds block scheduled disposal and ordinary erasure, expiration asks an authorized custodian, and approved disposal removes originals, previews, OCR, search vectors and connector caches with a journal. **Acceptance evidence:** Place a hold, request erasure and receive a refusal; release it with authority, approve disposal and prove all derivative downloads and searches stop.

- **FIRM-007 — Operate and recover the owned system.** The principal owns deployment and provider accounts, sees storage/connector costs and health diagnostics that name each connector’s authorization and token expiry, last successful sync, queued and failed jobs, scheduled-job lag, storage use against quota, last verified backup and restore test, bank-feed staleness and unreconciled trust periods, each with its next action. The principal exports full archives with large attachments and reconciled trust history. Restore validates counts and hashes, keeps secrets local and leaves external sending and charging paused until reauthorized. **Acceptance evidence:** Restore a populated target firm into a new installation, reconcile attachment hashes and trust totals, expire a connector token and stop the bank feed and see each named in diagnostics with its next action, and prove no imported job sends or charges.

- **FIRM-008 — Define custom roles and rate visibility.** Beyond FIRM-002’s built-in roles, the principal defines custom roles with per-area permissions: billing rates (none, own, all), operating and trust bank accounts (none, balances only, full transactions), reports (none, own matters, firm-wide), contacts, documents, billing and settings. Server-side handlers apply the role to every read, export, search result and report; a role change takes effect on the next request and is audited. Legal approval and trust authorization stay tied to FIRM-002’s lawyer and trust-officer authority, and a custom role cannot confer them. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a role with own-rate visibility and no trust bank access and assign it to an assistant; request another timekeeper’s rate through the timesheet, draft invoice, report and export routes and the IOLTA register directly and receive refusal or redaction; widen the role, repeat, and inspect the audit of both changes; refuse a custom role that grants trust authorization.

- **BASE-001 — Preserve Base guarantees through replacements.** Satchel retains native Base identity, imports, portability and erasure guarantees. Its custom archive/erase doors replace generic product export/erasure for matter records, including originals, review history, import journals and erasure fences; this replacement must grow with every new product record. **Acceptance evidence:** Through native sign-in and Satchel backup, restore and erasure routes round-trip every native record, omit local authority and refuse stale erased content.

### Intake and engagement

- **INTAKE-001 — Publish conditional intake forms.** Staff publish branded accessible forms with conditional questions, consent, secure attachments, spam controls and practice-specific fields. Every submission becomes a permissioned lead with source and receipt; sensitive replies never appear in public URLs or notification subjects. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a family-law inquiry with a conditional answer and attachment from a phone; inspect the private lead, receipt and consent history.

- **INTAKE-002 — Manage a lead pipeline.** Staff qualify, assign and move leads through editable stages, keep referral source, consultation outcome and next action, detect duplicates and report stalled or lost leads with reasons. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign two prospects, merge a duplicate with preserved provenance, miss a follow-up and inspect the owner’s due queue and conversion report.

- **INTAKE-003 — Book consultations.** Prospects book approved availability with timezone conversion, buffers, fees if required, reminders and reschedule/cancel links; consultation records and intake answers reach the responsible lawyer without duplicate calendar bookings. **Acceptance evidence:** Book the last available slot, race a second booking, reschedule across timezones and verify one appointment and the linked intake record.

- **INTAKE-004 — Maintain a legal contact graph.** People and organizations have aliases, former names, related entities, addresses, communication consent and matter roles such as client, adverse party, insurer, witness, expert and counsel. Deduplication preserves relationships and conflict history. **Acceptance evidence:** Import two aliases of an insurer, relate it to two matters, merge the duplicate and search every relationship without losing source identities.

- **INTAKE-005 — Review conflicts across the whole firm.** Conflict review searches former and prospective clients, related entities, aliases, staff relationships and closed matters; it records possible matches, counsel’s disposition, waivers and engagement restrictions without exposing walled detail to unauthorized people. **Acceptance evidence:** Search a former client’s alias and a prospective adverse affiliate, route a restricted match to its custodian and require recorded counsel disposition before engagement.

- **INTAKE-006 — Execute engagement agreements.** Counsel selects a versioned fee agreement, reviews client and payer details, requests electronic signatures through DocuSign or Inkwell, tracks signer identity, decline/expiry and completion, and files the signed document and certificate with the matter. **Acceptance evidence:** Send an hourly engagement to two signers, receive a signed webhook twice and inspect one completed agreement and certificate; a declined agreement cannot open an engaged matter.

- **INTAKE-007 — Convert a lead without losing history.** An authorized lawyer converts a cleared, accepted engagement into a matter, carrying parties, signed terms, files, consultation notes, assigned staff and approved retainer request; idempotent conversion never opens a second matter. **Acceptance evidence:** Convert a signed lead twice through intake and inspect one matter with complete linked history and an uncharged retainer request.

- **INTAKE-008 — Follow up prospects under consent.** Owners configure consultation reminders, unanswered-inquiry follow-ups and engagement chases by channel, quiet hours and contact limits. A reply, opt-out, conflict stop or decline halts the sequence and assigns the next human action. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule a consented lead sequence, receive a reply after its first reminder, restart the worker and verify later sends stop and responsibility remains visible.

### Matter operations

- **MATTER-001 — Model practice-specific matters.** Each practice area has editable matter types, custom typed fields, case numbers, courts, opponents, insurance limits, fee arrangements and required records. Changes are versioned and validated without rewriting historic closed matters. **Acceptance evidence:** Create litigation and estate templates, require different fields, import a case number and reject an invalid typed value.

- **MATTER-002 — Track stages and milestones.** Lists and kanban boards filter matters by lawyer, practice, stage, risk and next milestone. Stage transitions enforce required evidence and show blocked or overdue work without concealing exceptions. **Acceptance evidence:** Move a matter to demand-ready with a missing medical bill, see the blocker, attach the bill and complete the transition with an activity record.

- **MATTER-003 — Assign dependent work.** Tasks have owners, due dates, priorities, subtasks, dependencies, recurring schedules and completion evidence. Reassignment, absence coverage and bulk updates preserve history and do not silently change confirmed legal dates. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign dependent discovery tasks, reassign an absent assistant, complete a prerequisite and inspect remaining blockers and notifications.

- **MATTER-004 — Run editable matter workflows.** A versioned practice workflow generates tasks, events, document requests and draft documents from intake or approved stage transitions. The firm previews changes, chooses which open matters receive them and can stop a run without deleting completed work. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview a litigation workflow on an existing matter, approve selected steps, restart and verify each step is created once; edit the template without changing completed history.

- **MATTER-005 — Keep a complete matter timeline.** Matter notes, calls, email, documents, tasks, deadlines, client commitments, bills and payments appear in a filterable chronology with source links and scoped mentions. Private notes remain excluded from client views and unauthorized search. **Acceptance evidence:** Add a private strategy note and shared progress note, file an email and inspect lawyer and client timelines for the correct entries.

- **MATTER-006 — Manage staff capacity and handoff.** Supervisors see assigned workload, due work, absence coverage and unowned matters; a handoff transfers selected responsibilities with acknowledgment while preserving original timekeeper attribution and access policy. **Acceptance evidence:** Hand off an absent lawyer’s active matters, acknowledge the assignment and confirm every imminent task has an owner with no change to historic time entries.

- **MATTER-007 — Close and reopen matters deliberately.** Closure checks unfinished deadlines, client promises, final bills, trust balances and original-file return; counsel resolves or explicitly carries exceptions. Reopening records its reason and restores only approved workflows and access. **Acceptance evidence:** Attempt closure with a trust balance and open court task, resolve or authorize exceptions, close and reopen while retaining the entire history.

- **MATTER-008 — Number matters by firm scheme.** The principal configures the firm’s matter-number pattern from a starting number, padding, year, client number, practice-area code and separators, with optional per-client sequences. Each new matter receives the next unique number atomically, even when two open at once; a pattern change applies only to later matters, and imported matters keep their source numbers (MOVE-002) without colliding with new ones. Matter numbers appear on invoices, documents, calendar entries and search. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a year, client number and four-digit sequence pattern starting at 1050, open two matters concurrently and receive consecutive distinct numbers; import a Clio matter numbered 2019-0042 and confirm the next new matter does not collide; change the pattern and verify existing numbers are unchanged on the matter, its invoice and its documents.

- **MATTER-009 — Search the whole firm from one box.** One search box finds matters by name, matter number or case number, contacts and aliases, notes, tasks, events, documents, email, invoices and payments the person may see, grouped by type with keyboard navigation and a phone layout. Results apply matter access, custom roles and ethical walls before any title, count or snippet is returned and link to the record; DOC-003 supplies document and email full text. **Acceptance evidence:** Search a client surname and find its matter, contact, note, task and invoice in grouped results; search a matter number and a case number; run the same queries as a walled-out principal and an unassigned assistant and receive no walled or unassigned record, title, count or snippet.

### Documents and evidence

- **DOC-001 — Store real working files.** Upload resumable large PDFs, DOCX, images, audio/video and bulk folders with per-file limits shown before transfer, quotas, malware quarantine and hash-verified originals. A 500 MiB scanned record and a multi-gigabyte matter set are ordinary supported work, not export-only attachments. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a 500 MiB record, interrupt and resume, then bulk-upload a matter folder; download matching hashes and refuse access during quarantine.

- **DOC-002 — Preview and compare versions.** Staff preview PDFs, images and office documents, annotate separately from originals and compare versions with author/time provenance. Concurrent saves create explicit conflicts; source citations continue to identify the reviewed version. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a PDF preview, annotate it, save conflicting Word edits and compare versions while downloading the unchanged original.

- **DOC-003 — Search OCR and full text.** OCR scanned records and index permitted document text, metadata and email with matter/date/type filters, exact page snippets and index freshness. Search applies matter walls before snippets and includes deletion of all derivatives on disposal. **Acceptance evidence:** Find a phrase only present in a scanned page, open its citation, revoke access and confirm snippets and cached results disappear.

- **DOC-004 — Organize and act on document sets.** Folders, tags, document types, saved views, bulk filing and duplicate detection support everyday matter navigation. Bulk operations preview their destinations and permissions and retain original identity and version history. **Acceptance evidence:** File a mixed production into two matter folders, catch a duplicate and bulk-retag it without exposing private files or replacing originals.

- **DOC-005 — Work in Microsoft Word.** Open matter DOCX files in Word through Microsoft 365, preserve tracked changes, comments and original versions, and save a new version back to the correct matter with conflict detection and access revocation. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a matter brief in Word, edit tracked changes, save back, race another save and inspect a visible conflict and both retained versions.

- **DOC-006 — Assemble legal documents and forms.** Versioned Word/PDF templates merge typed matter fields, conditional clauses and repeating parties from approved questionnaires. Staff preview unresolved fields, maintain court-form versions and approve the generated DOCX/PDF before sending. **Acceptance evidence:** Assemble a two-party engagement and a jurisdiction form, change one questionnaire answer, compare revisions and refuse delivery with an unresolved required field.

- **DOC-007 — Sign and file matter documents.** DocuSign and Inkwell connectors request ordered or parallel signatures on an approved exact version, remind permitted recipients and retain signed bytes, certificate, provider ID and failure/decline history. Revoking a request never overwrites a signed original. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a settlement release, decline then resend a revised version, complete it and replay the callback; inspect one signed version per completed request.

- **DOC-008 — File Outlook email and attachments.** Microsoft 365/Outlook users file individual messages or approved threads and attachments to matters, retain sender/recipient headers and original .eml, associate sent replies and prevent duplicate filing across staff. Filing never grants a new matter reader. **Acceptance evidence:** File an Outlook thread with an attachment, reply from Outlook, replay sync and inspect one linked message per provider ID with correct matter permissions.

- **DOC-009 — File Gmail email and attachments.** Google Workspace/Gmail users select matter labels or messages for filing, preserve original headers, threads and attachments, associate outbound replies and handle deleted or revoked mail access without losing filed evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** File a labeled Gmail conversation, add a reply, disconnect its grant and verify retained originals and a visible stopped-sync state.

- **DOC-010 — Connect SharePoint OneDrive and Drive.** Microsoft SharePoint/OneDrive and Google Drive connectors map selected folders and file versions to matters, track remote IDs and permission drift and offer controlled copy or linked-file custody. A revoked remote link is reported, never presented as a retained original. **Acceptance evidence:** Map folders from each service, update and revoke a remote file, and inspect the new version, failed access and explicit copy-versus-link custody.

- **DOC-011 — Connect Box and Dropbox.** Firms map existing Box and Dropbox folders to matters with least-privilege grants, version references, sync diagnostics and permission reconciliation; neither service becomes an alternative firm access boundary. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect each provider, map one restricted folder, change remote permissions and confirm unauthorized clients and staff cannot acquire its contents.

- **DOC-012 — Build medical and bill chronologies.** Authorized teams organize received medical records and bills by provider, date, treatment, charge and source page, identify gaps and duplicates, and reconcile totals without silently deciding causation or altering source evidence. Health-data access, agreements and processor configuration are explicit setup work. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a treatment packet and overlapping bill, review the source-linked chronology and discrepancy totals, and prevent publication before counsel approves it.

- **DOC-013 — Prepare controlled evidence productions.** Counsel selects matter documents, privilege labels, redactions and Bates numbering; productions retain a manifest, privilege log, recipient and delivery proof while preserving unredacted originals and permitting recall of still-unopened shares. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a redacted production, review its privilege log, deliver to approved opposing counsel and inspect exact delivered bytes and the original retained separately.

### Calendar and legal delivery

- **CAL-001 — Schedule matter appointments.** Matter calendars support appointments, recurring events, attendees, locations/video links, timezones, shared availability and cancellation history alongside distinct legal deadlines. Moving an appointment never moves an unrelated court deadline. **Acceptance evidence:** Book a recurring conference across daylight saving, invite two staff, cancel one instance and compare matter and firm views.

- **CAL-002 — Synchronize Google Calendar both ways.** Selected Google calendars exchange event creation, edits, cancellations, attendees and recurrence with stable IDs, loop prevention, conflict resolution and visible token/failure state; matter confidentiality controls event detail. **Acceptance evidence:** Create an event in each system, edit concurrently, cancel a recurring instance and reconnect; inspect one consistent series with conflicts shown.

- **CAL-003 — Synchronize Outlook calendars both ways.** Microsoft 365/Outlook calendars support the same two-way lifecycle, delegated calendars and attendee changes, with subscription renewal, missed-change recovery and explicit choice of which details leave the matter. **Acceptance evidence:** Edit a delegated Outlook appointment while Satchel is disconnected, reconnect and reconcile its updated attendees and cancellation without duplicate invitations.

- **CAL-004 — Calculate LawToolBox deadlines.** Through a contracted LawToolBox integration, counsel chooses court, jurisdiction, trigger and service method, reviews computed dates and rule citations, and confirms publication to dependent tasks and calendars. Coverage and provider rule versions are visible. **Acceptance evidence:** Use a covered court and service trigger in a provider sandbox, review holiday-adjusted dates, refuse an unsupported court and publish only after attorney confirmation.

- **CAL-005 — Calculate CalendarRules deadlines.** CalendarRules is a supported alternative rules connector with court coverage, trigger/service inputs, rule versions, calculation provenance and attorney confirmation. Switching providers compares proposed dates rather than silently replacing confirmed deadlines. **Acceptance evidence:** Calculate one covered court schedule, compare it with an existing schedule and confirm differences before calendar changes; unsupported coverage leaves explicit manual review.

- **CAL-006 — Propagate reviewed source changes.** A changed court order, service date or rules version presents affected dates and dependent tasks with old/new source passages. Counsel accepts or rejects each legal change; approved calendars and assignees then reconcile and acknowledge it. **Acceptance evidence:** Replace an order, inspect impacted deadlines, refuse automatic legal adoption, approve one change and verify all connected calendar copies and acknowledgment states.

- **CAL-007 — Remind and escalate deadlines.** Rules define advance reminders, channels, quiet hours and escalation to a backup lawyer; acknowledgment differs from completion, and completion needs evidence of the underlying act. Missed or uncertain delivery stays visible until resolved. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule a deadline reminder, fail one delivery, restart before escalation and complete the deadline with a filing receipt; only that receipt closes the obligation.

- **CAL-008 — Show firm deadline coverage.** Lawyers see firm and matter agenda, upcoming limitation dates, conflicts, unconfirmed dates and absence coverage. Reports identify each source, owner, confirmation status and next action; an extracted date never appears as confirmed. **Acceptance evidence:** View overlapping hearings and an unconfirmed limitations date, assign coverage and export the agenda with source and confirmation columns.

- **CAL-009 — File and serve through InfoTrack.** An authorized lawyer approves exact filing documents, court, service recipients and fees; InfoTrack submits through covered networks and returns acceptance/rejection, stamped copies, service proof and costs to the matter. Submission alone is never filing completion. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a sandbox filing, receive rejection, correct and resubmit with a new revision, then reconcile stamped acceptance, service proof and expense without duplicate charges.

- **CAL-010 — Track court docket changes.** Connect PACER through an authorized docket provider such as CourtListener RECAP where available and court/provider accounts for coverage, attach returned docket entries and orders, and propose related work with a lawyer reviewing legal effects and provider fees. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive an amended order twice, inspect one docket entry and source artifact, and require counsel review before changing a legal deadline.

- **CAL-011 — Remind staff of appointments and tasks.** Each staff member receives in-app and email reminders, and push reminders under MOBILE-002, for their appointments and tasks at firm-default or per-item offsets, with snooze and dismiss. Reminders follow reassignment, cancellation and rescheduling, fire once across restarts and carry no confidential detail outside the authenticated app. A person who relies on Google or Outlook reminders for synced events can choose calendar-only reminders, so the same event never reminds twice. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a task due tomorrow with a one-hour reminder and an appointment with a 15-minute reminder; reassign the task, reschedule the appointment and restart the worker; verify the new owner alone receives one task reminder, the appointment reminder fires once at the new time and the email carries only a link and a minimal subject; set calendar-only for a synced event and verify Satchel sends no reminder for it.

### Client communication

- **CLIENT-001 — Invite a secure client portal.** A client or authorized co-client signs in to only expressly shared matters, with separate payer access and revocable delegates. Staff preview the client view; internal notes, other clients and privileged drafts never leak through search or notifications. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite two co-clients and a payer, share one release, revoke a delegate and exercise direct portal file/message URLs for each identity.

- **CLIENT-002 — Retain secure two-way messages.** Portal conversations retain matter, participants, attachments, delivery/read states and staff assignments. Recipients receive minimal notifications and open confidential content inside the authenticated portal. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a confidential attachment, receive a client reply, inspect the retained thread and unread queue and verify email notifications contain no confidential body.

- **CLIENT-003 — Chase document requests to receipt.** Staff request specific records with instructions, owner, due date and acceptance criteria. Clients upload securely, staff accept or explain deficiencies, and authorized reminders continue only until accepted, stopped or escalated. **Acceptance evidence:** Request two records, receive an incomplete upload, send a permitted clarification and close only the accepted item while the second remains due.

- **CLIENT-004 — Give clients tasks and events.** The portal shows shared upcoming events, client tasks, due dates, completion instructions and acknowledgments; clients cannot mark a lawyer’s legal deadline complete or change confirmed court dates. **Acceptance evidence:** Share a preparation task and hearing, complete the client task from a phone and refuse a client attempt to alter the hearing deadline.

- **CLIENT-005 — Text clients and leads with Twilio.** Two-way SMS/MMS through the firm’s registered Twilio number retains conversations on the correct lead/matter, consent, delivery/failure and opt-outs. A guided setup walks the principal through connecting or creating the Twilio account, buying or porting a number, US A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration prefilled from firm settings, approval tracking and a test message; texting stays disabled with a visible reason until registration is approved. Quiet hours and recipient confirmation are part of setup; sensitive content uses portal links. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete the guided setup through sandbox registration states and see texting stay disabled while the campaign is pending; after approval text a consenting lead, file a reply, process STOP and verify pending reminders halt without exposing private message content.

- **CLIENT-006 — Deliver and reconcile email.** Connected Microsoft/Gmail or the firm’s configured transactional mail provider sends reviewed client correspondence and invoice notifications, retains provider references and bounce/complaint states and avoids blindly resending an uncertain delivery. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a reviewed letter, simulate a bounce and lost acknowledgment at the provider edge, and inspect one attempt with a visible next human action.

- **CLIENT-007 — Keep call records and authorized recordings.** Staff log calls, participants, advice notes and follow-ups and link recordings/transcripts obtained through a consented phone provider. Recording permission and US state consent policy are checked before recording; staff approve transcript-derived work. **Acceptance evidence:** Log a call and task, connect a consented recording and refuse capture without the required consent, then inspect permissioned transcript access.

## Honest boundaries

Satchel is US-first and uses USD by default. Licensed lawyers decide legal interpretation, conflicts, advice, filings and jurisdiction-specific professional duties. The software supports evidence, review and execution controls; it does not act as counsel or guarantee conflict clearance. Licensed Westlaw/LexisNexis/vLex access supplies external legal authority; restricted licensed content is not silently copied or redistributed.

A regulated US bank owns each IOLTA/operating account. LawPay or a firm-verified Stripe arrangement processes payments; provider onboarding, trust protections, ACH authorization and bank agreements are required engineering and setup, not excluded scope. Satchel still owns the complete legal ledger, approval workflow, three-way reconciliation and discrepancy resolution. It neither takes custody of client money nor claims that a payment request is settlement.

LawToolBox/CalendarRules provide contracted court-rule content; counsel confirms results and resolves uncovered courts through source-cited manual review. InfoTrack and authorized courts provide filing/service networks and stamped results. Satchel owns orchestration, returned evidence and follow-up; provider outages or rejected filings remain open work. Health records and agent processing require the firm’s applicable data/health agreements, subprocessors and access policy before processing; implementing those agreements is part of delivery.

Microsoft 365/Word and Google Workspace remain editing and mail platforms; Box/Dropbox remain repositories. Satchel supplies integrated matter filing, versions, search and permissions, rather than rebuilding desktop Word or a general enterprise e-discovery hosting network. Matter evidence productions and source-cited drafts stay in scope. US tax and trust treatment is configured and reviewed by the firm’s lawyer/accountant; Satchel records calculation provenance and does not guarantee professional compliance by installation alone.
