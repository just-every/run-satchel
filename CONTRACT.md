# Satchel product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Satchel is the US small-firm practice management system a Clio Manage, MyCase, Smokeball or PracticePanther buyer can switch to, with Filevine’s matter-work depth and source-grounded assistance beyond today’s Eve workflows. It runs intake and conflicts, engagements, matters, documents and email, court deadlines, client communication, time capture, billing, built-in trust accounting, legal payments and collections.

This is the product being built towards. Missing implementation is a gap, never a reason to narrow the product. Each named clause describes buyer behavior and an acceptance journey through a public screen, route, download, command or real provider boundary. Current status is judged separately from that target. ACCEPTANCE.md lists the same journeys and statuses for build rounds. No product code, approved homepage, UI or behavior changes in this documentation round.

## Whole job and authority

Satchel owns the legal practice loop from prospective client through closed matter and reconciled money. Trust accounting is built in: client subsidiary ledgers, bank registers and three-way reconciliation are core product obligations, not a link to an external spreadsheet. Banks and contracted processors hold and move actual money. Counsel retains conflict clearance, legal judgments, deadline confirmation and external filing authority. Agents prepare and act only under explicit delegated nonlegal authority; people retain legal and financial approvals.

The published foundation had no root CONTRACT.md or named clause IDs. SAT-001 through SAT-013 give stable names to its existing ACCEPTANCE.md promises, preserving the behavior. Their 1 MiB implementation limit and old exclusions of delivered invoices, trust accounting and provider calls describe the prior release, not target boundaries. New target clauses supersede those scope exclusions while preserving original-byte integrity, review history and authority checks. USD cents remain exact; foreign-currency invoices require explicit agreements and separate currency ledgers.

Neighboring products remain separate. [Chest](https://github.com/just-every/run-chest) owns general-purpose file collaboration; Satchel owns matter evidence and connects repositories. [Inkwell](https://github.com/just-every/run-inkwell) supplies signatures, alongside DocuSign; Satchel owns engagement and matter workflow. [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) is a general accounting product; Satchel owns legal billing and IOLTA subsidiary accounting and connects QuickBooks Online/Xero. General CRM, enterprise e-discovery and unrestricted legal research databases remain separate products; matter intake, scoped research integration and evidence production remain Satchel obligations.

All clauses inherit server-side authorization, matter confidentiality, stable source identities, append-only posted financial records, reviewed exact revisions, durable job state and explicit uncertain outcomes. Native Base session/import/portability guarantees continue through BASE-001. A fresh production installation contains no sample clients. Examples in acceptance journeys are test inputs, never seeded production records. Paid/network services may use official sandboxes at the edge; application workflows must use real application handlers.

## Published foundations

- **SAT-001 — Invite a firm team.** The principal saves and invites lawyers and assistants, deactivates membership and controls matter grants. Private handlers check the native session, active firm role and matter access themselves; a hidden button is never the boundary. **Acceptance evidence:** Through Team and the JSON API invite an assistant, grant one matter, read it, then revoke membership; direct private reads and writes fail immediately.

- **SAT-002 — Review fresh conflicts before opening.** A principal or lawyer searches firm parties, adverse parties and aliases, reviews recorded matches and writes a conclusion before opening a matter. Any party insert, update or delete invalidates the search; linking already-known, unchanged parties to a new matter does not. Each conflict search clears one opening only, so two separately reviewed openings that involve only existing, unchanged parties may both succeed concurrently. Search is evidence for counsel, never a guarantee of legal clearance. **Acceptance evidence:** Run a conflict search, change a party record, attempt opening against the stale search and receive refusal; repeat with fresh review and explicit confirmation to open. Open two separately reviewed matters involving only existing, unchanged parties concurrently and both succeed; reusing either search is refused.

- **SAT-003 — Grant matter access.** The principal grants or removes access to individual matters. Other staff see only assigned matters; the principal can see all. Finance and ethical-wall extensions in FIRM-002 and FIRM-003 add narrower authority without claiming they exist today. Its grant door refuses staff excluded by an INTAKE-005 engagement restriction, naming it (proved under INTAKE-005). **Acceptance evidence:** As an unassigned assistant request a matter and its billing, files and deadlines directly, receive refusal, then grant and remove access and repeat.

- **SAT-004 — Record exact USD work.** Authorized staff record dated matter time in whole minutes and hourly USD cents, or disbursements in USD cents. Charges use exact integer arithmetic with declared half-up rounding to cents; invalid dates and unsafe amounts are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Record 90 minutes at $250 per hour and a $25 disbursement through Time & bills; inspect $400 in the prepared package and reject an invalid date.

- **SAT-005 — Review immutable bill exports.** A lawyer prepares a bill from unbilled matter entries, reviews the exact revision and exports its recorded CSV artifact. Source reservations prevent double billing; assistants and agents cannot approve, and an exported artifact cannot be voided in place or overwritten: a later BILL-016 void is a separate append-only record beside it. **Acceptance evidence:** Reserve entries in a draft, refuse another bill using them, refuse premature export and assistant approval, approve as counsel and download the immutable CSV.

- **SAT-006 — Correct by reviewed additions.** A correction is a separately reviewed delta referencing the original bill and source amounts. The original exported artifact, review identity and history remain intact. A delta adjusts an amount without releasing entries; when the entries themselves are wrong (matter, rate, narrative or template), BILL-016 voids the invoice and rebills them. **Acceptance evidence:** Create and approve a courtesy reduction through the billing API; export its negative delta and download the original again with identical bytes. BILL-015 cutover is the principal’s first saved scheme. Every pre-cutover number and approved/exported file stays byte-identical; a legacy draft with a reservation issues under that same number. Later drafts have only their ID and a non-invoice label. Number assignment, exact approved-revision and tax gates, PDF and LEDES rendering, permanent firm-wide registration and counter advance form one atomic issue operation; a failed batch consumes none, collisions are refused rather than skipped, and no gap-free claim is made. Scheme versions apply only to later issues; year is from issue time in the firm IANA timezone and a counter reset requires an explicit noncolliding next number. Corrections retain <original>-R<k> without advancing the counter. Imports preserve source numbers and provenance with the next counter above every source ordinal; restore retains the register, counter and files.

- **SAT-007 — Keep private original versions.** Matter documents retain original bytes, hashes, version metadata and creator history. Matter visibility follows access; private visibility is uploader/principal only. Downloads validate integrity and use safe attachment headers. The current implementation limit is 1 MiB; DOC-001 widens the target. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload private and matter-visible originals, refuse the assistant private download, append a version and retrieve both originals through authenticated file routes.

- **SAT-008 — Confirm source-linked deadlines.** A deadline has a date, IANA timezone, source citation and optional exact document version. A lawyer confirms and completes it with evidence. A changed source version supersedes earlier live deadlines and requires fresh review. **Acceptance evidence:** Propose and confirm a cited deadline, append its source version, refuse completion of the superseded date, then confirm a replacement and record delivery evidence.

- **SAT-009 — Let agents prepare for people.** Revocable agent credentials read only within their human sponsor’s access and prepare matter, bill and deadline proposals. A person approves or declines once; approving a proposal does not substitute for conflict, bill or deadline review. **Acceptance evidence:** Use an agent key to prepare each proposal, refuse agent approval and repeated human decisions, check the human decision record, then revoke the key and refuse access.

- **SAT-010 — Preview Clio contact move-in.** The principal previews the documented Clio contacts CSV through Base import-kit, commits its exact fingerprint, resumes and inspects the provenance journal. Replay reuses the job; undo removes unchanged unused contacts and preserves native matter links. Full Clio migration is MOVE-002. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload fixtures/clio-contacts.csv through Move in, prove preview writes nothing, refuse a changed fingerprint, apply and replay, open a linked matter and undo while preserving its contact.

- **SAT-011 — Restore a private firm archive.** The principal exports the complete current native firm archive and restores only into empty product data after integrity validation. Product records, review history and import provenance round-trip; identity, sessions, local configuration and agent keys remain excluded. **Acceptance evidence:** Export through Team & safety, refuse corrupt and nonempty restore, restore into an empty installation, export again and compare all native rows and original bill bytes. BILL-015 cutover is the principal’s first saved scheme. Every pre-cutover number and approved/exported file stays byte-identical; a legacy draft with a reservation issues under that same number. Later drafts have only their ID and a non-invoice label. Number assignment, exact approved-revision and tax gates, PDF and LEDES rendering, permanent firm-wide registration and counter advance form one atomic issue operation; a failed batch consumes none, collisions are refused rather than skipped, and no gap-free claim is made. Scheme versions apply only to later issues; year is from issue time in the firm IANA timezone and a counter reset requires an explicit noncolliding next number. Corrections retain <original>-R<k> without advancing the counter. Imports preserve source numbers and provenance with the next counter above every source ordinal; restore retains the register, counter and files.

- **SAT-012 — Erase originals with a restore fence.** A principal confirms a document name and reason to erase every version’s bytes while preserving metadata, hashes and the erasure journal. Supplying the later journal fences older archives; copies already downloaded or delivered remain outside local erasure. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase through the document API, refuse assistant erasure, confirm all version downloads fail and reject a pre-erasure backup when accompanied by the later journal.

- **SAT-013 — Keep the chosen product surfaces.** The public site retains Run Satchel at /run and navigable Markdown guides at /docs. The installed root remains the Lobby; sign-in, setup and all workspace tabs retain the picked theme and available actions at desktop and phone widths. **Acceptance evidence:** Navigate the built site’s run instructions and guides; at 1440 and 390 pixels complete sign-in, setup and each workspace tab with real matter records, checking actions and role restrictions.

- **OSS-001 — Free and open source, MIT licensed.** Satchel’s source is publicly available under the MIT license, with no Satchel license fee, subscription, paywall, seat charge or matter limit. A firm runs it on its own computer or server, including its own Cloudflare account. Payment, e-signature and accounting providers, including LawPay, Stripe, DocuSign, Inkwell, QuickBooks Online and Xero, bill the firm directly under their own terms, as do hosting, mail, SMS, court-rule and filing providers; “100% free” describes Satchel software, not those provider services. **Acceptance evidence:** Read `LICENSE` and the homepage, /run, README and owner guides and verify they agree on MIT, free Satchel software, self-hosting and direct provider billing. Follow the published run command on a fresh installation and complete the native matter/time/bill workflow without any Satchel payment, plan or license prompt. Before connecting a provider inspect its terms and direct firm-billing disclosure and prove Satchel adds no software or provider-resale charge.

## Target clauses

### Firm authority and records

- **FIRM-001 — Set up an empty US firm.** A fresh installation starts with the buyer’s firm, offices, US contact details, practice areas, IANA timezone, USD billing defaults and fiscal settings entered by the principal or imported with provenance: imported from a Satchel firm-settings JSON file (the one **Download firm settings** produces), recorded with its file name and SHA-256; a full firm archive restore also carries the settings and their provenance. Importing firm settings from Clio, MyCase and other products belongs to MOVE-002 to MOVE-007. Production never starts with demo clients. **Acceptance evidence:** Start a fresh installation, finish setup, inspect empty matters and enter firm settings; download them and import the file into a fresh installation, where the source shows its file name and SHA-256; restore a full firm archive and find the same settings and provenance.

- **FIRM-002 — Separate finance and legal rights.** Principal manages membership and policies; lawyers clear conflicts, confirm legal deadlines and approve legal work; assistants manage assigned work without legal approval; billing staff draft and deliver approved bills; trust officers authorize transfers; accountants reconcile with read-only matter context; clients see explicit portal grants. **Roles and client sharing (ct approved 22 October 2026).** Billing staff draft bills and, once a lawyer or the principal has approved one, issue and export it; they cannot approve, reach trust records, conflicts, membership or matter access. Accountants read their assigned matters, billing and every trust record and reconcile (import a statement period, record bank clearance, match checks) but cannot authorize, sign or reopen a period or change anything else. A client reaches no staff door: only the principal shares one matter at a time with an active client member, with a recorded reason and an audit entry, and the client then sees that matter’s number, title and status; a walled matter is never shown and withdrawing the share closes the view. Agent keys and integrations need a principal, lawyer, assistant or trust-officer sponsor, so a member whose role changes to billing, accountant or client loses any earlier key at once. FIRM-008’s custom roles narrow these roles and never widen them; CLIENT-001 to CLIENT-009 own portal identities, content and messaging beyond this view and inherit the share and withdrawal rules. **Acceptance evidence:** Create each role and use direct APIs for legal approval, invoice delivery, trust transfer, reconciliation and access changes; deny every capability outside that role. Also: as billing staff draft and issue an approved bill but fail to approve it; as an accountant reconcile and fail to authorize, sign or change a matter; as a client see only a shared matter, with every staff door refused.

- **FIRM-003 — Enforce ethical walls.** Restricted matters support explicit ethical walls, excluding ordinary administrators and search, exports, notifications, connected repositories and agent context. A designated custodian records exceptional access with reason and an audit entry. Before shipping, inherit REPORT-001’s exclusion of a principal outside the wall from every report row, count, drill, option and CSV. The principal establishes a wall with an active lawyer or principal custodian and explicit members; subsequent membership changes belong only to the custodian. Exceptional access requires the custodian’s recorded reason, expires within 60 minutes, can be revoked and never delegates to agents or integrations. Membership changes revoke outstanding exceptional grants. Current matter doors, search, reports, notifications and exports intersect wall access; an indivisible firm archive, import original or account aggregate is refused unless every included wall permits the reader. DOC-010, DOC-011 and DOC-014 inherit the same boundary before connected repositories or desktop caches ship. **Wall authority (ct 22 Oct 2026).** The principal establishes a wall naming an active lawyer or principal custodian, who is always a wall member; for a wall INTAKE-010 creates at opening, the default custodian is the lawyer who records the restriction. After creation only the current custodian changes membership, hands custodianship to another active lawyer or principal inside the wall, or dissolves the wall, each with a recorded reason; nobody outside the wall, the principal and administrators included, adds themselves, changes members or custodian, approves access or dissolves it. With no active custodian the wall stays in force, reads "custodian unassigned", allows no exceptional access, and an active lawyer or principal inside the wall may take custodianship with a recorded reason. Exceptional access is requested with a reason by one active person in a native session (never an agent key, integration, portal user or client contact). Only the current custodian approves or refuses it, with a decision note. It lasts at most 60 minutes from approval and never renews: a further need is a new request. It is revoked early by the custodian or by any membership or custodian change. It opens the walled matter's current doors for that person only. It never confers membership or custodian authority, never reaches their agent keys or integrations, and never counts toward the every-wall permission for whole-firm archives, import originals or account aggregates. Request, decision, expiry, revocation and the recipient's walled reads under a grant are FIRM-005 audit actions readable inside the wall; the recipient sees their own request, decision and expiry. INTAKE-010's walled routing and the walls its restrictions create are proved under INTAKE-010. **Acceptance evidence:** Wall off an adverse matter, search and export as a principal outside the wall, then approve a documented emergency access and inspect its trace. Also (ct 22 Oct 2026): as the principal outside the wall, be refused adding yourself, changing members, re-designating the custodian, dissolving the wall and approving your own request; as an agent key and an integration, be refused requesting or using exceptional access. As the custodian, approve the principal's request, see it refused after 60 minutes, approve another and see it revoked by a membership change and again by handing custodianship to an inside lawyer. Under an active grant, be refused a whole-firm archive. Deactivate the custodian and see "custodian unassigned" with no grant possible until an inside lawyer takes custodianship.

- **FIRM-004 — Secure sessions and connected secrets.** Native sign-in is extended with MFA or federated SSO, session/device revocation and least-privilege connector grants. Current outbound data declaration: CONNECT-001 lets only the principal explicitly enable a public HTTPS webhook destination and selected matter metadata events (stable matter/record/event IDs, type and time; no original bytes or private titles). Its Worker fetch refuses credentials, fragments, reserved names, non-public literal addresses and redirects; there is no separate DNS preflight or application address pin. Resolved-address/pinned egress (Base egress door) is inherited here by name and keeps CONNECT-001 partial until adopted. Secrets stay installation-local, encrypted at rest, redacted from logs and absent from archives; revocation stops new work and exposes in-flight uncertainty. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in with MFA, revoke a device and a Microsoft connector while work is queued, then attempt reads and inspect logs and an export.

- **FIRM-005 — Export a complete action audit.** Authorized custodians filter and export an append-only history of actor, role, matter, source, revision, human decision, automation policy and provider outcome. Audit access respects walls; corrections add records rather than rewriting prior actions. The principal is the current native custodian. Every capability and provider that exists today records this evidence; FIRM-002, FIRM-003, FIRM-004, FIRM-008, BILL-005/006/007, BOOKS-001/002, CONNECT-003 and AGENT-010 inherit the obligation before their new roles, walls, providers, policies or doors ship, as does each future clause adding a capability or record kind. Legacy rows retain every original field unchanged; unavailable role, channel, source and revision fields remain null and are labeled “legacy, not recorded”, never inferred from present membership. Audit date filters include whole UTC calendar days, ordered by action timestamp and ID. Export streams a frozen append-only snapshot with its record count; its own export-request action is recorded after that snapshot. Archive restore retains all imported rows unchanged and appends a new custody action naming the exact supplied archive hash. **Acceptance evidence:** Open the audit, filter a matter and date range, export a transfer and approval trail, and verify an unauthorized reader cannot inspect it.

- **FIRM-006 — Apply retention and legal holds.** Counsel (the principal or a lawyer on the matter) places and releases legal holds and sets matter and document retention schedules; holds block scheduled disposal and ordinary erasure. Until FIRM-002 and FIRM-008 define a custodian role, expiration asks the principal, who approves disposal. Approved disposal removes originals and every derivative, and its journal counts each kind removed: today Satchel keeps no previews, OCR text, search vectors or connector caches, so the journal records zero of each; when DOC-002 (previews), DOC-003 (OCR and search index) and DOC-010 (connector caches) add derivatives, disposal must remove them and the journal must count them. DOC-002’s separate annotations and retained conflict originals are also removed and counted individually. DOC-012 chronology snapshots and reviews and DOC-013 production packets and manifests are removed when any source is disposed, and the journal counts affected chronologies and productions. Before a lead-retention door ships, FIRM-006 must also apply its holds, disposal, original and derivative removal and restore-fence obligations to INTAKE-001 inquiry records and R2 originals. A disposed document keeps only a tombstone (name, erasure time and journal) in its matter’s document list: no download returns any version, and no document search, saved view search or duplicate check finds it. INTAKE-005 waivers and restriction records are retained conflict originals. **Acceptance evidence:** As a lawyer on the matter place a hold, request erasure and receive a refusal; release it, let the schedule expire and approve disposal as the principal (a lawyer or assistant cannot approve it); prove all downloads and searches stop and the disposal journal counts every derivative kind removed (zero previews, OCR text, search vectors and connector caches today).

- **FIRM-007 — Operate and recover the owned system.** The principal owns deployment and provider accounts, sees storage/connector costs and health diagnostics that name each connector’s authorization and token expiry, last successful sync, queued and failed jobs, scheduled-job lag, storage use against quota, last verified backup and restore test, bank-feed staleness and unreconciled trust periods, each with its next action. The principal exports full archives with large attachments and reconciled trust history. Restore validates counts and hashes, keeps secrets local and leaves external sending and charging paused until reauthorized. **Acceptance evidence:** Restore a populated target firm into a new installation, reconcile attachment hashes and trust totals, expire a connector token and stop the bank feed and see each named in diagnostics with its next action, and prove no imported job sends or charges. On a fresh computer run `npx -y @runeditrun/rer dev just-every/run-satchel`, reach the local installation, then follow the owner deployment guide to run it in the firm’s own server/Cloudflare account without a Satchel subscription.

- **FIRM-008 — Define custom roles and rate visibility.** Beyond FIRM-002’s built-in roles, the principal defines custom roles with per-area permissions: billing rates (none, own, all), operating and trust bank accounts (none, balances only, full transactions), reports (none, own matters, firm-wide), contacts, documents, billing and settings. Server-side handlers apply the role to every read, export, search result and report; a role change takes effect on the next request and is audited. Legal approval and trust authorization stay tied to FIRM-002’s lawyer and trust-officer authority, and a custom role cannot confer them. Before shipping, apply those report scopes to every REPORT-001 row, count, drill and CSV. **Exact semantics.** A custom role is a named ceiling the principal places on one active member who is not a client or the principal, beside their built-in role: it lowers what that role could reach and never raises it, so legal approval, conflict clearance, trust authorization, matter grants and ethical walls stay exactly as FIRM-002 and FIRM-003 define them, and a role that names trust authorization or legal approval is refused. Rates: none, own (entries, drafts and bill lines the member recorded, plus agreements naming no timekeeper) or all; every other timekeeper’s rate, amount and discount, and every frozen CSV, LEDES or XML text, is withheld and marked, never shown as zero, while bill totals remain, and exports that carry rates need all. Bank: none, balances (account and subsidiary-ledger balances only) or full; a bill row shows no trust custody without full. Reports: none, own (matters whose responsible attorney is the member and leads assigned to them, applied before every row, count, drill, option and CSV) or firm-wide, always inside current grants and walls. Contacts, documents, billing and settings: none, read (reads only) or write (the built-in role decides). Every server door of a session, an agent key and its proposals, and an integration key is assigned to its areas in one map the principal can read, and a door missing from it is refused to a narrowed member. A change applies at the next request; the audit keeps actor, role, previous permissions and each assignment. FIRM-004, DOC-010, DOC-011, DOC-014, REPORT-002, REPORT-003, BOOKS-001, BOOKS-002, BILL-005 to BILL-016 and every new door inherit the map: each must be given an area before it ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a role with own-rate visibility and no trust bank access and assign it to an assistant; request another timekeeper’s rate through the timesheet, draft invoice, report and export routes and the IOLTA register directly and receive refusal or redaction; widen the role, repeat, and inspect the audit of both changes; refuse a custom role that grants trust authorization.

- **BASE-001 — Preserve Base guarantees through replacements.** Satchel retains native Base identity, imports, portability and erasure guarantees. Its custom archive/erase doors replace generic product export/erasure for matter records, including originals, review history, import journals and erasure fences; this replacement must grow with every new product record. **Acceptance evidence:** Through native sign-in and Satchel backup, restore and erasure routes round-trip every native record, omit local authority and refuse stale erased content.

### Intake and engagement

- **INTAKE-001 — Publish conditional intake forms.** Staff publish branded accessible forms with conditional questions, consent, secure attachments, spam controls and practice-specific fields. Every submission becomes a permissioned lead with source and receipt; sensitive replies never appear in public URLs or notification subjects. The principal and lawyers publish immutable numbered form versions with the firm name, optional PNG/JPEG logo, practice label, plain-text review consent and typed text, choice, date or yes/no questions. A conditional question depends on one earlier choice or yes/no answer; the server refuses hidden answers and validates required visible answers. Publication names an active principal, lawyer or assistant as the lead assignee. A submission requires explicit consent for receipt and review of that inquiry only: it never matches a contact identity, changes channel consent or authorizes sending. The public page uses strict-origin referrers and exact-origin submissions; a random, hashed 20-minute token binds the published revision, a honeypot and at most ten accepted submissions per form/network/UTC hour limit spam. Only an hourly HMAC network digest is retained, for at most two hours; scheduled cleanup removes expired security data. One accepted submission atomically records one native lead, source form/version, immutable answers and consent evidence, original-file references and an opaque receipt; replay returns the same receipt, never a second lead. The receipt is displayed after POST, with no anonymous read-back page, email or notification. At most three PDF, PNG or JPEG originals of up to 10 MiB each (10,485,760 bytes) are stored in R2 after byte-signature/type checks; they are labeled not malware-scanned, never rendered inline, and authenticated attachment downloads verify their hashes. Each private read and download applies INTAKE-002 visibility to both the original lead and every survivor after a merge; merging never grants the survivor assignee access to an invisible source original. R2 originals and immutable provenance round-trip in streamed native archives; credentials, spent tokens, network digests and temporary upload reservations do not. Interrupted upload reservations expire after twenty minutes for cleanup; current uploads ask an archive request to wait rather than exporting incomplete originals. INTAKE-007 inherits complete inquiry provenance and the prohibition on sensitive URL/notification-subject content; DOC-001 carries larger/resumable uploads and quarantine, while FIRM-006 must add lead-original retention, holds, disposal and restore fencing before its lead-retention door ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a family-law inquiry with a conditional answer and attachment from a phone; inspect the private lead, receipt and consent history.

- **INTAKE-002 — Manage a lead pipeline.** Staff qualify, assign and move leads through editable stages, keep referral source, consultation outcome and next action, detect duplicates and report stalled or lost leads with reasons. Staff record a lead with the prospect’s name, optional email and phone, inquiry summary and referral source; the referral source and creator are fixed provenance. Until INTAKE-001 forms ship, staff record leads by hand; each INTAKE-001 submission must become one of these leads before it ships. The principal and lawyers see and maintain every lead and configure the ordered pipeline stages; an assistant sees and works only leads assigned to them, records new leads assigned to themselves and cannot reassign or merge; trust officers see no leads. Assignees are active lawyers, assistants or the principal. A lead is qualified (pending, qualified or not qualified), moved between stages, and keeps a consultation outcome and a next action with an optional date. Each change is checked against the lead’s revision and appended to its immutable history. A possible duplicate is another open lead with the same email (case-insensitive), the same phone digits (at least seven) or the same name (ignoring case and spacing). Counsel merges a duplicate into a surviving lead: the survivor keeps its own fields, and the duplicate stays as a merged record with its source, referral, creator and history, shown as the survivor’s provenance. Losing a lead requires a reason. Converting links a matter opened through the conflict-reviewed door that the person can see, one lead per matter; INTAKE-007 adds full carry-over and MATTER-004’s opening trigger applies to that matter. Each person’s due queue lists their own open leads whose next-action date is on or before the firm-local today, flagged overdue when it is earlier. An open lead is stalled when its next action is overdue or when its last activity was 14 or more firm-local calendar days ago (inclusive). A missing next-action date alone is not stalled. Lead names and details never appear in notification subjects; INTAKE-001, INTAKE-007 and every future lead notification door inherit this rule before shipping. The conversion report excludes merged duplicates and counts leads, open, converted and lost leads by referral source and by stage, with conversion shown as converted ÷ leads in whole percent, rounded half-up. It lists stalled leads with their reasons and lost leads with their recorded reasons, and each person’s report covers only the leads they can see. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign two prospects, merge a duplicate with preserved provenance, miss a follow-up and inspect the owner’s due queue and conversion report.

- **INTAKE-003 — Book consultations.** Prospects book approved availability with timezone conversion, buffers, fees if required, reminders and reschedule/cancel links; consultation records and intake answers reach the responsible lawyer without duplicate calendar bookings. **Acceptance evidence:** Book the last available slot, race a second booking, reschedule across timezones and verify one appointment and the linked intake record.

- **INTAKE-004 — Maintain a legal contact graph.** People and organizations have aliases, former names, related entities, addresses, communication consent and matter roles such as client, adverse party, insurer, witness, expert and counsel. Deduplication preserves relationships and conflict history. The principal or a lawyer maintains the graph; assistants and trust officers read only contacts attached to permitted matters, with only permitted matter-role links. Firm contact access does not grant matter detail. Stable source party IDs and import provenance remain separate from the surviving contact ID. A person explicitly classifies both contacts, chooses a same-kind survivor and records a reason; a recorded exact preview binds that person, role and graph revision and applies once. Every physical source ID, profile, alias, former name, address, related-entity link, matter role and recorded conflict snapshot remains intact; a losing ID resolves to its survivor, without rewriting historical matter or financial text. Consent evidence is append-only per source identity and channel (email, phone, SMS and post), unknown by default. The latest recorded decision for each identity controls, in durable recording order; the entered evidence date is an attestation, not a scheduled change. Any denied identity makes the merged channel denied; otherwise all identities must allow or it stays unknown. A merge grants no messaging or sync authority: INTAKE-008, INTAKE-009, CLIENT-005 and CLIENT-006 must enforce their own current authority before shipping their new doors. **Acceptance evidence:** Import two aliases of an insurer, relate it to two matters, merge the duplicate and search every relationship without losing source identities.

- **INTAKE-005 — Review conflicts across the whole firm.** Conflict review searches former and prospective clients, related entities, aliases, staff relationships and closed matters; it records possible matches, counsel’s disposition, waivers and engagement restrictions without exposing walled detail to unauthorized people. Search evidence records the number of canonical contacts actually scanned and all physical source identities, including merged identities. An explicit client role on a permitted open matter is labeled current client; on a permitted closed matter it is labeled former client, with the year of that matter’s current closure event in the firm IANA timezone (UTC before setup). These are recorded matter associations, not a determination that professional duties ended. ## Honest boundaries

Satchel is US-first and uses USD by default. Licensed lawyers decide legal interpretation, conflicts, advice, filings and jurisdiction-specific professional duties. The software supports evidence, review and execution controls; it does not act as counsel or guarantee conflict clearance. Licensed Westlaw/LexisNexis/vLex access supplies external legal authority; restricted licensed content is not silently copied or redistributed.

A regulated US bank owns each IOLTA/operating account. LawPay or a firm-verified Stripe arrangement processes payments; provider onboarding, trust protections, ACH authorization and bank agreements are required engineering and setup, not excluded scope. Satchel still owns the complete legal ledger, approval workflow, three-way reconciliation and discrepancy resolution. It neither takes custody of client money nor claims that a payment request is settlement.

LawToolBox/CalendarRules provide contracted court-rule content; counsel confirms results and resolves uncovered courts through source-cited manual review. InfoTrack and authorized courts provide filing/service networks and stamped results. Satchel owns orchestration, returned evidence and follow-up; provider outages or rejected filings remain open work. Health records and agent processing require the firm’s applicable data/health agreements, subprocessors and access policy before processing; implementing those agreements is part of delivery.

Microsoft 365/Word and Google Workspace remain editing and mail platforms; Box/Dropbox remain repositories. Satchel supplies integrated matter filing, versions, search and permissions, rather than rebuilding desktop Word or a general enterprise e-discovery hosting network. Matter evidence productions and source-cited drafts stay in scope. US tax and trust treatment is configured and reviewed by the firm’s lawyer/accountant; Satchel records calculation provenance and does not guarantee professional compliance by installation alone.
