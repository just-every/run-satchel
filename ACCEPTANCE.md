# First release

- A principal invites lawyers and assistants, grants matter access, and a lawyer opens a matter only after recording and reviewing a fresh conflict search.
- The team records USD time and disbursements; a lawyer reviews and exports a bill package once per source, with separately reviewed corrections preserving the original.
- The team keeps private or matter-visible original document versions and cited deadlines; a lawyer confirms and completes deadlines, and changed source versions require fresh review.
- An agent prepares matter, bill and deadline proposals; a person approves or declines them, with the decision recorded.
- A principal previews and imports documented-shape Clio contacts using Base import-kit, inspects its journal and undoes unchanged contacts while preserving contacts used by matters.
- A principal exports and restores the whole firm archive into an empty installation, and erases document bytes with an erasure journal that fences older backups.

Not needed: trust accounting or funds movement, tax calculations, invoice delivery,
e-filing, legal research or advice, guaranteed conflict clearance, provider calls,
brand design, a run site or domain purchase. Files are limited to 1 MiB each.

The static run site has a Run Satchel action to `/run` and navigable `/docs`
guides rendered from repository Markdown. An installed `/` remains the Lobby.
Sign-in, first-run setup and all workspace tabs share the picked Satchel theme
at desktop and phone widths, preserving every existing action and role boundary.
